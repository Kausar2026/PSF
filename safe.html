🍎 Why Fruit Quality Matters

Fresh and high-quality fruits are rich in vitamins, minerals, fibre, and antioxidants. They help improve immunity, support digestion, maintain heart health, and provide natural energy. Choosing fresh fruits also ensures better taste, nutrition, and overall well-being.

⚠️ Harmful Effects of Chemicals

  • ❌ Excess pesticides may remain on fruit surfaces.
  • ❌ Artificial ripening chemicals can reduce nutritional value.
  • ❌ Chemical residues may cause allergies and stomach problems.
  • ❌ Long-term exposure may affect overall health.
  • ✅ Always wash fruits thoroughly before eating.

💧 How to Wash Fruits Properly

  • 🚰 Wash fruits under clean running water for 20–30 seconds.
  • 🍎 Gently rub the fruit surface with your hands.
  • 🚫 Never use soap, detergent, or bleach.
  • 🧻 Dry fruits with a clean cloth or tissue.
  • 🔪 Wash fruits first, then cut them using a clean knife.

🧊 Safe Storage Tips

  • 🥶 Store soft fruits in the refrigerator.
  • 🍌 Keep bananas at room temperature.
  • 🍎 Separate ripe and unripe fruits.
  • 📦 Store fruits in clean containers.
  • ❌ Remove spoiled fruits immediately.

🥗 Healthy Eating Tips

  • 🍽️ Eat 2–3 servings of fruits every day.
  • 🌈 Choose fruits of different colours.
  • ⏰ Eat fruits in the morning or as a healthy snack.
  • 💧 Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • 🚫 Avoid eating damaged or mouldy fruits.

🚫 Fruits to Avoid

  • ❌ Rotten or mouldy fruits.
  • ❌ Fruits with a bad smell.
  • ❌ Fruits with deep cuts or leakage.
  • ❌ Fruits showing insect damage.
  • ❌ Overly bruised fruits.

🌍 Protect the Environment

  • ♻️ Reduce food waste.
  • 🛍️ Use reusable shopping bags.
  • 🌱 Compost fruit peels whenever possible.
  • 🍃 Buy local and seasonal fruits.
  • 💚 Support eco-friendly farming.

💡 Did You Know?

  • 🍊 Vitamin C helps strengthen immunity.
  • 🍌 Bananas provide instant natural energy.
  • 🍎 Apples are a good source of fibre.
  • 🍇 Grapes contain powerful antioxidants.
  • 🥭 Seasonal fruits are usually fresher and more nutritious.
