Freshness You Can Trust

🫐 About Blackberry

Blackberry is a small, juicy, and highly nutritious fruit that is rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, dietary fiber, manganese, and powerful antioxidants. It helps strengthen the immune system, improve digestion, support heart health, promote healthy skin, and protect the body from harmful free radicals. Blackberries are low in calories and can be enjoyed fresh, in smoothies, fruit salads, desserts, or jams.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 🛡️ Rich in Antioxidants
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 💪 Boosts Immunity
  • 🧠 Supports Brain Health
  • 🍽️ Improves Digestion
  • ✨ Keeps Skin Healthy

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 43 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 10 g
  • Fiber: 5.3 g
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin K
  • Manganese

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • 🏃 Before Workout
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store in the refrigerator.
  • Wash only before eating.
  • Keep in a covered container.
  • Consume within 3–5 days.
