🍎 About Apple

Apple is one of the most popular and nutritious fruits in the world. It is rich in vitamins, fiber, and antioxidants that help keep the body healthy. Apples come in different varieties such as Red, Green, and Golden apples, each with its own unique taste and benefits.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 🌾 Rich in Dietary Fiber
  • 🛡️ Good Source of Vitamin C
  • 🍽️ Improves Digestion
  • ⚡ Gives Natural Energy
  • 🧠 Rich in Antioxidants

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 52 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 14 g
  • Fiber: 2.4 g
  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium
  • Water: 86%

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • 🏃 Before Workout
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Keep in a cool and dry place.
  • Refrigerate for longer freshness.
  • Wash before eating.
  • Avoid damaged or bruised apples.
