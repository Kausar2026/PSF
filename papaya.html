🍈 About Papaya

Papaya is a tropical fruit that rich in vitamins A, C, and E, fiber, folate, and antioxidants. It contains an enzyme called papain, which helps improve digestion and supports gut health. Papaya strengthens the immune system, promotes healthy skin, supports eye health, and helps reduce inflammation. It is best enjoyed fresh, in fruit salads, smoothies, or juices.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 🍽️ Improves Digestion
  • 🛡️ Boosts Immunity
  • ✨ Keeps Skin Healthy
  • 👁️ Supports Eye Health
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • ⚡ Provides Natural Energy

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 43 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 11 g
  • Fiber: 1.7 g
  • Vitamin A
  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 After Breakfast
  • 🍽️ Before Lunch
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Keep unripe papaya at room temperature.
  • Refrigerate after ripening.
  • Wash well before cutting.
  • Consume within 2–3 days after ripening.
