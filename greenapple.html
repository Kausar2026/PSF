🍏 Green Apple

Freshness You Can Trust

🍏 About Green Apple

Green Apple is a crunchy, slightly sour, and highly nutritious fruit. It is rich in vitamin C, dietary fiber, antioxidants, and potassium. Green apples help improve digestion, support weight management, strengthen the immune system, regulate blood sugar levels, and promote healthy skin and heart health. They are best eaten fresh as a snack, in salads, or as juice.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 🌾 Rich in Dietary Fiber
  • 🛡️ Rich in Vitamin C
  • 🍽️ Improves Digestion
  • ⚡ Provides Natural Energy
  • ✨ Helps Maintain Healthy Skin

  • 🏃 Before Workout
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store in a cool, dry place.
  • Refrigerate for longer freshness.
  • Wash before eating.
  • Avoid bruised or damaged fruits.
