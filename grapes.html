🍇 About Grapes

Grapes are small, sweet, and juicy fruits that grow in clusters on vines. They are rich in vitamin C, vitamin K, potassium, dietary fiber, and powerful antioxidants such as resveratrol. Grapes help improve heart health, strengthen the immune system, support digestion, keep the body hydrated, and protect cells from damage caused by free radicals. They also provide natural energy and promote healthy skin. Grapes can be eaten fresh or used in juices, salads, smoothies, raisins, and desserts.

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store grapes in the refrigerator.
  • Wash only before eating.
  • Keep them in a ventilated container.
  • Consume within 5–7 days for best freshness.
