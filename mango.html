🥭 About Mango

Mango is known as the "King of Fruits" because of its delicious taste and rich nutritional value. It is an excellent source of vitamin A, vitamin C, vitamin E, dietary fiber, potassium, and antioxidants. Mango helps strengthen the immune system, improves digestion, supports eye health, promotes healthy skin, and provides natural energy. It is a refreshing tropical fruit that can be enjoyed fresh, in juices, smoothies, desserts, and fruit salads.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 👁️ Supports Healthy Eyes
  • 🛡️ Boosts Immunity
  • 🍽️ Improves Digestion
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • ✨ Good for Healthy Skin

  • Carbohydrates: 15 g

  • Vitamin C
  • Potassium

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Afternoon
  • 🍴 After Breakfast
  • 🏃 Before Workout

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store unripe mangoes at room temperature.
  • Keep ripe mangoes in the refrigerator.
  • Wash before eating.
  • Consume within 2–3 days after ripening.
