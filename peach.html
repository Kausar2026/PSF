🍑 About Peach

Peach is a sweet, juicy, and fragrant fruit that is rich in vitamins A and C, dietary fiber, potassium, and antioxidants. It helps improve digestion, supports healthy skin, boosts the immune system, promotes heart health, and keeps the body hydrated. Peaches are low in calories and make a refreshing and nutritious snack. They can be enjoyed fresh, in fruit salads, smoothies, desserts, or juices.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 🛡️ Rich in Vitamin C
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 👁️ Good for Eye Health
  • 🍽️ Supports Healthy Digestion
  • ✨ Promotes Healthy Skin
  • 💧 Helps Keep the Body Hydrated

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 39 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 10 g
  • Fiber: 1.5 g
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin A
  • Potassium

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • 🍽️ After Breakfast
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store unripe peaches at room temperature.
  • Refrigerate after ripening.
  • Wash before eating.
  • Consume within 3–5 days after ripening.
