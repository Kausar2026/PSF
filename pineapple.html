🍍 About Pineapple

Pineapple is a sweet, juicy, and tropical fruit that is rich in vitamin C, manganese, dietary fiber, and bromelain, a natural enzyme. It helps improve digestion, boosts the immune system, supports heart health, reduces inflammation, and keeps the body hydrated. Pineapple also promotes healthy skin and provides natural energy. It can be enjoyed fresh, as juice, in fruit salads, smoothies, or desserts.

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🍴 After Lunch
  • 🏃 Before Workout

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store whole pineapple at room temperature for 1–2 days.
  • Refrigerate after cutting.
  • Keep in an airtight container.
  • Consume within 3–5 days after cutting.
