About Pomegranate

Pomegranate is a sweet and juicy fruit that is rich in vitamins C and K, dietary fiber, potassium, folate, and powerful antioxidants. It helps boost the immune system, supports heart health, improves digestion, increases hemoglobin levels, and protects the body from harmful free radicals. Pomegranate also promotes healthy skin and provides natural energy. It can be enjoyed fresh, as juice, in salads, or desserts.

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store whole pomegranates in a cool, dry place.
  • Refrigerate for longer freshness.
  • Keep seeds in an airtight container.
  • Consume seeds within 4–5 days.
