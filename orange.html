🍊 About Orange

Orange is a juicy and refreshing citrus fruit that is rich in vitamin C, dietary fiber, potassium, folate, and antioxidants. It helps strengthen the immune system, improves digestion, supports heart health, keeps the body hydrated, and promotes healthy skin. Oranges are low in calories and provide natural energy, making them an excellent fruit for daily consumption. They can be eaten fresh or used in juices, fruit salads, smoothies, and desserts.

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • 🍴 After Breakfast
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store at room temperature for a few days.
  • Refrigerate to keep fresh longer.
  • Wash before peeling or eating.
  • Avoid storing in direct sunlight.
