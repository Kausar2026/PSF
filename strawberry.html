🍓 About Strawberry

Strawberry is a bright red, sweet, and juicy fruit that is rich in vitamin C, manganese, dietary fiber, folate, and powerful antioxidants. It helps boost the immune system, supports heart health, improves digestion, promotes healthy skin, and protects the body from free radicals. Strawberries are low in calories and high in water content, making them a delicious and healthy choice. They can be enjoyed fresh, in fruit salads, smoothies, desserts, or juices.

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Wash only before eating.
  • Keep in a ventilated container.
  • Consume within 2–3 days for best freshness.
