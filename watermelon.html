Freshness You Can Trust

🍉 About Watermelon

Watermelon is a refreshing and juicy fruit that is rich in water, vitamins A and C, and antioxidants. It helps keep the body hydrated, supports healthy skin, improves digestion, and is a perfect fruit during hot summer days. Watermelon is naturally sweet, low in calories, and enjoyed by people of all ages.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 💧 Keeps the Body Hydrated
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 🛡️ Rich in Vitamin C
  • 👁️ Good for Eye Health
  • ⚡ Refreshes and Gives Energy
  • ✨ Supports Healthy Skin

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 30 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 8 g
  • Fiber: 0.4 g
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin A
  • Water: 91%

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • ☀️ Afternoon
  • 🏃 After Workout

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store whole watermelon at room temperature.
  • Refrigerate after cutting.
  • Keep cut pieces in an airtight container.
  • Consume within 3–4 days after cutting.
