🍓 Raspberry

Fresh • Safe • Healthy

🍓 About Raspberry

Raspberry is a delicious berry rich in vitamins, antioxidants and fibre. It helps improve immunity, supports heart health and promotes healthy digestion. Raspberries are low in calories and are an excellent choice for a healthy diet.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • ❤️ Boosts immunity
  • 🧠 Improves brain health
  • 🩸 Supports heart health
  • 🥗 Rich in dietary fibre
  • ⚡ Gives natural energy
  • 🛡️ High in antioxidants

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories : 52 kcal
  • Vitamin C : 26 mg
  • Fibre : 6.5 g
  • Potassium : 151 mg
  • Carbohydrates : 12 g

⏰ Best Time to Eat

Morning breakfast, evening snack or after exercise.

🧊 Storage Tips

  • Keep refrigerated.
  • Wash only before eating.
  • Consume within 2–3 days.

⚠️ Note

Wash thoroughly before eating and avoid spoiled berries.
