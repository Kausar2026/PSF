🫐 About Blueberry

Blueberry is a small, sweet, and nutritious fruit known for its rich antioxidant content. It is packed with vitamins C and K, fiber, and essential minerals that help improve brain function, support heart health, strengthen immunity, and promote healthy skin. Blueberries are low in calories and can be enjoyed fresh, in smoothies, desserts, or salads.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 🧠 Improves Brain Health
  • 🛡️ Rich in Antioxidants
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 👁️ Good for Eye Health
  • 💪 Boosts Immunity
  • ✨ Keeps Skin Healthy

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 57 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 14.5 g
  • Fiber: 2.4 g
  • Vitamin C
  • Vitamin K
  • Manganese

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Mid-Morning Snack
  • 🏃 Before Workout
  • 🌇 Evening Healthy Snack

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store in the refrigerator.
  • Wash only before eating.
  • Keep in a covered container.
  • Consume within 5–7 days.
