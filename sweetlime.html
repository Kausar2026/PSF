Freshness You Can Trust

🍋 About Sweet Lime

Sweet Lime, also known as Mosambi, is a juicy and refreshing citrus fruit that is rich in vitamin C, dietary fiber, potassium, and antioxidants. It helps boost the immune system, improves digestion, keeps the body hydrated, supports healthy skin, and provides instant energy. Sweet Lime is low in calories and is commonly enjoyed as fresh fruit or juice, especially during hot weather.

❄️ Storage Tips
