Freshness You Can Trust

🥑 About Avocado

Avocado is a creamy, nutrient-rich fruit that is packed with healthy fats, fiber, vitamins E, C, K, and B6, as well as potassium and folate. It supports heart health, improves digestion, promotes healthy skin, helps control cholesterol levels, and provides long-lasting energy. Avocado can be eaten fresh, in salads, sandwiches, smoothies, or as guacamole.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 🧠 Improves Brain Function
  • 👁️ Good for Eye Health
  • ✨ Keeps Skin Healthy
  • 💪 Rich in Healthy Fats
  • 🍽️ Supports Healthy Digestion

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • Calories: 160 kcal
  • Carbohydrates: 9 g
  • Fiber: 7 g
  • Healthy Fats: 15 g
  • Potassium
  • Vitamin E

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • 🌞 Morning
  • 🥗 Breakfast
  • 🍽️ Lunch
  • 🏃 Before Workout

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Keep unripe avocados at room temperature.
  • Refrigerate after ripening.
  • Store cut avocado with lemon juice.
  • Consume within 2–3 days after cutting.
