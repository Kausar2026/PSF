🍑 About Plum

Plum is a sweet and juicy fruit rich in vitamins, antioxidants, and dietary fiber. It supports digestion, boosts immunity, and helps keep the body healthy.

💪 Health Benefits

  • ✔ Rich in Vitamin C and Vitamin K
  • ✔ Improves digestion
  • ✔ Supports heart health
  • ✔ Strengthens immunity
  • ✔ Good source of antioxidants

🥗 Nutrition (Per 100g)

  • ✔ Calories: 46 kcal
  • ✔ Carbohydrates: 11.4 g
  • ✔ Protein: 0.7 g
  • ✔ Fat: 0.3 g
  • ✔ Fiber: 1.4 g
  • ✔ Vitamin C: 9.5 mg
  • ✔ Vitamin K: 6.4 µg
  • ✔ Potassium: 157 mg

⏰ Best Time to Eat

  • ✔ Morning or evening is the best time to eat plums.
  • ✔ Eat 1–2 hours after breakfast or lunch.
  • ✔ Avoid eating too many plums on an empty stomach if you have acidity.
  • ✔ 2–3 plums per day are enough for most people.

📦 Storage Tips

  • ✔ Keep unripe plums at room temperature.
  • ✔ Refrigerate ripe plums to keep them fresh longer.
  • ✔ Wash before eating.
