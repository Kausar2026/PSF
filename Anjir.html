🟣 About Anjir

Fig, also known as Anjir, is a sweet and nutritious fruit that is rich in dietary fiber, calcium, potassium, iron, magnesium, and antioxidants. It helps improve digestion, strengthens bones, supports heart health, regulates blood sugar levels, and boosts the immune system. Figs provide natural energy and are beneficial for overall health. They can be eaten fresh or dried and are commonly used in desserts, smoothies, and healthy snacks.

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store fresh figs in the refrigerator.
  • Wash before eating.
  • Keep in a covered container.
  • Consume within 2–3 days for best freshness.
