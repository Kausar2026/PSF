🍒 About Cherry

Cherry is a small, sweet, and nutritious fruit rich in vitamins C and A, potassium, fiber, and antioxidants. It helps reduce inflammation, supports heart health, improves sleep quality due to its natural melatonin content, and strengthens the immune system. Cherries are also beneficial for healthy skin and muscle recovery. They can be eaten fresh, dried, or used in juices, desserts, and smoothies.

🌿 Health Benefits

  • 🛡️ Rich in Antioxidants
  • ❤️ Supports Heart Health
  • 💪 Boosts Immunity
  • 😴 May Improve Sleep Quality
  • 🍽️ Supports Healthy Digestion
  • ✨ Promotes Healthy Skin

❄️ Storage Tips

  • Store cherries in the refrigerator.
  • Wash only before eating.
  • Keep in a covered container.
  • Consume within 5–7 days.
